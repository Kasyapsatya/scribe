## Question

Explain the method selections for GL


## Scribe

For General Liability (both Gross and Net bases), method weightings rely entirely on incurred-based methods across all accident-year bands, shifting from pure experience-based methods in mature years to expectation-based methods in recent green years (`Methods_and_Weights` tab):

* **1995–2014**: **100% Incurred development** (col 4)
* **2015–2020**: **50% Incurred BF + 50% Incurred development** (col 8 & col 4)
* **2021–2022**: **50% Incurred BF + 50% Incurred Cape Cod** (col 8 & col 12)
* **2023**: **100% Incurred BF** (col 8)
* **2024**: **50% Incurred BF + 50% Incurred loss ratio** (col 8 & col 16)

*Note: All references to Bornhuetter-Ferguson ("BF") reflect Incurred BF.*

### Interpretation
* **Zero Weight on Paid Methods**: Paid-based techniques (Paid Development col 6, Paid BF col 10, Paid Cape Cod col 14) receive 0% weight across all accident years, reflecting exclusive reliance on incurred loss & legal data.
* **Gradual Transition to Expectations**: As accident years become less mature, weights transition from 100% Incurred Development (AY 1995–2014) to a blend with Incurred BF (AY 2015–2020), introducing Incurred Cape Cod (AY 2021–2022), 100% Incurred BF (AY 2023), and finally a 50% Incurred loss ratio blend (AY 2024) to stabilize volatile green-year indications.
* **Method Spread Sensitivity**: The spread between method indications relative to the selected ultimate peaks in transition and less mature years, notably **20.8%** in AY 2019, **17.5%** in AY 2022, and **14.0%** in AY 2023 (`Methods_and_Weights`).
